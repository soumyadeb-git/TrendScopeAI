Data quality & method

What is real vs. estimated

◎
01
Reported traffic

Approximate traffic buckets from RSS—not exact query counts.

02
RSS timestamp

Shown as the feed timestamp; it is not independently verified as the trend start time.

03
Traffic bucket changes

Compared with the previous successful snapshot. These are coarse bucket shifts, not exact growth percentages.

04
Local intelligence

Category labels and content plans are local heuristics. Scores are snapshot-relative, use available signals only, and are omitted when the feed lacks enough data.
